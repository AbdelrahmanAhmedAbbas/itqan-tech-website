## Design Context

Updated from Abdelrahman's website brief, 1 November 2026. This replaces the previous startup/SaaS positioning.

### Users

CEOs, founders, SMB owners and agency owners, Visitors arrive through LinkedIn, Upwork or Loom. Their operations run through spreadsheets, WhatsApp, email approvals and software that almost fits. They need an internal system built around the actual workflow. Engineering, construction, consulting and real estate are relevant proof matches. International English-first audience, with English/Arabic delivery and a later approved Gulf/RTL expansion.

Owner refinement: the home hero should welcome a broader audience for custom web/mobile apps, internal systems and AI automation. Do not assume every visitor uses spreadsheets or restrict the introduction to particular job titles or team sizes. Lead with “Custom software, built around your business.” Keep the Loom funnel and factual operations case studies.

### Offer and evidence

One offer: replace scattered operational work with a custom internal system and useful AI automation. Delivery is Blueprint → Build → Care. The primary conversion is a free personal 2-minute Loom. A smaller link offers a 15-minute call. Core En is live and used daily. Middlemen MDOS is in build, with planned go-live 23 Nov 2026; never automatically change that status. Wasfa is Itqan's own product, hidden by default. Use only approved facts. No invented metrics, testimonials, logos, prices, guarantees or client counts.

### Brand personality

Precise, practical, trusted. Calm confidence, short direct sentences, no hype. Make visitors feel understood and able to assess the next step. Itqan means mastery; show care through the work, not inflated claims.

### Aesthetic direction

A master's workshop: dark Void #09090B, restrained Itqan Blue #185ADB actions, warm sand #B88A5A / #D9B98F accents. Warm Paper #F7F5F2 offer and FAQ sections interrupt the dark rhythm. Inter body/UI, Playfair italic for brief editorial emphasis, JetBrains Mono only for small labels. The latest owner request explicitly restores real screenshots from the applications, with demo/QA data and no production staff or payroll data. Both white/light and dark website themes are required; preserve source screenshot colors. Generous space, fine borders, restrained corners and expressive motion throughout the page. The user explicitly requested extensive HyperFrames animation, scroll-driven screenshot layers and moving page elements. Keep reading text stable, support reduced motion and expose a global pause control.

### Design principles

1. The reader's workflow is the starting point.
2. Every page supports the same offer and next conversation.
3. Proof is factual; delivery status and unapproved evidence stay explicit.
4. Make scope, cost and next steps easy to understand.
5. Use accessible focus, keyboard controls, responsive layout and reduced-motion support.

### Implementation

React 19, Vite, React Router and static pre-rendering. English copy in src/i18n/en.json. Arabic copy awaits approval; do not show a switch to an unfinished locale. Links, prices and content flags live in src/site.config.ts. Booking is lazy-loaded. No third-party analytics is enabled. No automatic deployment.
